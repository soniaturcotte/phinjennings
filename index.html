Phin Jennings is a writer and researcher based in London.

He writes about artists, the art market and, occasionally, culture and politics more broadly. His work has appeared in publications including the Financial Times, Bloomberg, The Art Newspaper, Frieze and Air Mail.

He co-runs Abscondita, a publication about collecting, and regularly contributes exhibition reviews to The Fence’s Capital Letter.

He also works with artists, galleries and brands on selected projects. His experience ranges from writing catalogue notes to hosting panel discussions.

You can contact him on phineasjennings [at] gmail [dot] com, or find him on Instagram.
